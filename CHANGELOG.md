# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added

- Leave, expense, advance, purchase and travel requests with role-based approval and email approval links.
- Team leave calendar, yearly leave statements and pluggable public holiday calendars (Turkey included).
- Equipment and software inventory with assignment and handover confirmation.
- Incoming and outgoing invoices, agreements, jobs, monthly overview and reports with Excel export.
- Optional accounting emails with a monthly invoice bundle.
- Email and password authentication: first user becomes admin, admin approval or invite links for new users, password reset by email or admin link, rate limiting and CSRF protection.
- English and Turkish interface, per-user language, localized emails, dates, numbers and money.
- Workplace policy, privacy notice and acknowledgement templates (GDPR-style English, KVKK-style Turkish).
